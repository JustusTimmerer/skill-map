Skill-Map

Skills as a network of areas, categories, stations and projects

How it works

Your skills as a map

The Skill-Map sorts your skills into areas and categories and shows at which stations and in which projects you used them. Readers zoom in and filter by station, project and time range.

The server stores nothing. Your maps live only in this browser. “Export” saves a map as a file, and “Open map file” brings it back, also on another device.

The default set brings three areas with two categories each and two skills in each category: software development, data and ways of working. You change them and add stations and projects yourself. The empty map starts without areas.
