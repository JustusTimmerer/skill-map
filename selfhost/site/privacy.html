Du löschst eine Map mit „Löschen“ auf der Startseite, oder alle Maps, indem du die Website-Daten dieser Seite in deinem Browser löschst.

Overview

The Skill-Map is a tool that shows skills as a network. The website has no user accounts, no contact form, no analytics tools and no advertising.

No cookies

This website sets no cookies.

Your maps stay in your browser

The website stores the maps that you create or open here only in the local storage of your browser (localStorage). They are not sent to the server, and the server does not store them.

Besides the maps, the website keeps two more kinds of entries in the local storage: the language that you choose in the selection box, and, for each map, the last state of the force menu. The website needs this access to work, and it happens on your input (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). These entries do not leave your browser either.

An exported map file comes into being on your device. You decide where you store it and to whom you give it.

You delete a map with “Delete” on the start page, or all maps by deleting the site data of this site in your browser.

No content from other servers

The website loads nothing from other servers. The fonts and the program libraries are on this server.

The purpose is to deliver the website, to keep it secure and to find faults. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: the legitimate interest is a secure and stable operation.
